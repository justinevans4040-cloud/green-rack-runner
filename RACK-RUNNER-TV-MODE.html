Rack Runner TV Mode

Use HDMI from the Lenovo to the TV. No internet required.

OPEN RACK RUNNER
  1. Plug HDMI from Lenovo into the TV.
  2. Press Windows + P.
  3. Choose Duplicate for easiest setup.
  4. Open Rack Runner and press F11 for full screen.
  5. Use Ctrl + Plus / Ctrl + Minus to adjust size.
