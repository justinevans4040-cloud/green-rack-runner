Rack Runner TV Mode

Bar display helper for Green Rack Runner Functional V2. HDMI from the Lenovo to the TV. No internet required.

OPEN FUNCTIONAL V2
  1. Plug HDMI from Lenovo into the TV.
  2. Press Windows + P → Duplicate (easiest) or Extend.
  3. Open Green Rack Runner Functional V2.
  4. Press F11 for full screen on the TV display.
  5. Use Ctrl + Plus / Ctrl + Minus to adjust size.

Desktop shortcut “Rack Runner TV” launches this page, then the installed Functional V2 app.
